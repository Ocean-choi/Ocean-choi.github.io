Sese - se is a Korean children's hand-clapping game.

I'm a visual director working across spaces, fashion, and branding.
Based in Milan, reaching without borders.
sèsèsè.Lab is how I work.

About

Seajong Choi

Visual Director across Space, Fashion & Visual

Reading spaces, dressing brands,
speaking through images.

Founder of exhibition design studio SS STUDIO Ltd., running fashion group sèsèsè.Lab and snap photography service oggisnap, he leads space-based projects across Europe and Korea.

Asian brands looking to build their visual identity in Europe — this is where we start.
